Questions

Good to know.

Can my TalkTwin look and sound like me?

Yes, with your written permission and your own photo and voice sample. We never copy a real person's face or voice without their consent.

How long does it take?

Most single ads are ready within a few working days of you approving the presenter and voice. Seasonal rush orders, just ask.

Can you change the deal or logo later?

Yes. Swapping your logo, deal or call to action on an existing ad is quick and doesn't need a new recording.

What languages?

English for now, with local South African accents. Ask us about other languages.

Do I own the ads?

Yes, the finished ads are yours to post anywhere. We'll only show them in our portfolio if you say it's OK.
